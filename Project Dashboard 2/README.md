## Business Problem
Without a clear comparison between actual sales and budget, it was hard to know whether sales targets were being met each month, or which customers and products drove the most revenue.

## Solution
I stored and cleaned the data in MySQL, then built a three-page Power BI report that tracks sales vs budget by month, product, and customer. Slicers for year, month, city, category, and product make it easy to explore performance from different angles.

## Key Insights Delivered
- Sales vs budget performance, with the variance against target
- Monthly sales and budget trends
- Top 10 customers and top 10 products by sales
- Sales distribution by product category
- Geographic view of sales by customer city

## Dashboard Pages
- **Sales Overview:** sales vs budget, top customers and products, category split, and city map
- **Customer Details:** sales by customer and month
- **Product Details:** sales by product and month

## Tools & Skills
- **MySQL:** data storage, cleaning, and transformation
- **Power BI:** data modeling, visualization, and interactive slicers
