# Sales and Profit Dashboard (Pharmaceutical Industry)

An interactive Excel dashboard that turns 8,000+ raw sales records from 2021 to 2024 into clear insights on sales and profit by product, customer, region, and month.

## Business Problem
Sales data from 2021 to 2024 was stored in raw spreadsheets, making it hard to track sales and profit by product category, customer, region, and month.

## Solution
I cleaned 8,000+ records and built an interactive Excel dashboard using pivot tables, pivot charts, and slicers, showing key sales and profit insights at a glance.

## Key Insights Delivered
- Total sales and total profit
- Profit by year across product categories
- Sales by product
- Customer count by year
- Sales by state (map view)
- Monthly sales trend
- Top 5 customers by profit

## Interactive Filters
Slicers for **category**, **year**, and **month** update every chart in the dashboard.

## Tools & Skills
- **Excel:** data cleaning, pivot tables, pivot charts, slicers, and dashboard design

## Workflow
Raw Excel Data → Data Cleaning → Pivot Tables → Charts & Slicers → Excel Dashboard
